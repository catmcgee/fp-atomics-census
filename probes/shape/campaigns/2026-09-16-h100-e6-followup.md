The [planned C2 follow-up](NEXT_EXPERIMENTS.md) completed **two new records and
nine valid replay comparisons**. Every summary reproduces byte for byte offline

### Mixed-schedule intervention

The new record and all four replays set `TORCHINDUCTOR_DETERMINISTIC=1`,
`combo_kernels=false` and `benchmark_combo_kernel=false`. The mixed schedule
matches the historical default mixed schedule. Its warm positive control and
three independent cold replays were IDENTICAL.

This supplies a failing default control and a matching combined configuration
on the current GPU. It does not isolate any one of the three settings. The
negative control replays a historical record; the intervention uses a newly
created record on this pod. Record provenance therefore also differs between
arms. The small sample supports this combined configuration on the tested
schedule, without establishing a general remedy or identifying a kernel cause.

### TP=2: a material collective-backend qualification

Rank 1 independently reproduces rank 0's DIFFERS verdict and 494 differing rows.
Its hidden hashes match in 19/512 rows, full logits in 18/512 and free-running
argmax in 511/512. Hidden/logit divergence starts at pass 0, slot 0; the sole
argmax difference occurs at pass 19, slot 0. Teacher forcing preserves every
consumed token. Across the record and new replay, the rank-to-rank audit finds
four differing hidden-state rows, zero differing full-logit rows and zero
argmax differences.

**The historical record initialised FlashInfer's `mnnvl` workspace; all three
cold replays initialised `trtllm` after `CUDA_ERROR_ILLEGAL_STATE`.** This includes
the two historical M4 replays, as well as the new replay. The broad collective
backend names in E6 do not distinguish these subbackends. The verdicts are valid
whole-run observations, but they do not isolate cold-process repeatability
within one collective implementation.

The new replay matches the record's VBIOS as well as its recorded software
stack; it still falls back. Matching the preflight fields does not establish
multicast availability or the selected collective subbackend. The initialisation
logs also do not prove per-call kernel dispatch: vLLM applies tensor eligibility
and buffer gates afterwards. Despite the log's “norm fusion workspace” wording,
`fuse_allreduce_rms` resolved false. The source audit and exact log evidence are
retained in the [evidence directory](2026-09-16-h100-e6-followup/).
No backend is identified as the cause by these observations.

### MoE routing diagnostic

Both the new record and replay have `actual_router` counts on **all 37 forward
passes**, with 60 experts and top-4 routing. Every count sum equals the scheduled
token count multiplied by four. The eager replay is IDENTICAL.

This validates the Python wrapper when Python executes. It does not observe
routing inside captured CUDA graphs. The historical graph-enabled rows still
have null, unobserved counts; none is backfilled or interpreted as zero. These
uncompiled V1 runs do not retest the compiled MoE defect in issue #56900.

## Operational failures and recovery

Two failed orchestration attempts are retained separately from valid comparisons.

- After batch-invariant b completed and its evidence was verified, the original
  acknowledgement writer exposed a partially written JSON file. The runner
  stopped on a parse error. Recovery rechecked the handoff, copied files,
  immutable records, comparison and runner digests before marking this already
  completed arm acknowledged. It did not rerun the GPU arm. Subsequent writers
  close a `.pending` file and atomically rename it. The current runner also
  converts malformed acknowledgements to its normal STOP-producing error;
  that hardening postdates the executed runner.
- The first TP=2 attempt placed model files at `/root/hf` while runtime
  `HF_HOME=/root/hf` expected `/root/hf/hub`. It failed model resolution before
  inference. The layout was corrected, all pinned model files reverified and
  the exact vLLM offline model-path lookup checked. The successful retry used
  new results, cache and acknowledgement directories, preserving the failure.

The executed runner is retained with SHA-256
`1a1bad6ca3cdb810e1a36f5aa4f5464f7a31a21d9ac4a58070b2cd3dd49e0de6`.
Its source, per-arm handoffs, recovery evidence and frozen comparator are archived.
