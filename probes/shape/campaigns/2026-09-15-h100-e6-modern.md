# H100 campaign, 15 September 2026: repeated cold replay on vLLM 0.29.0

C2 recorded ten configurations and completed 37 comparisons on four H100 pods. All
comparisons reproduce offline with zero validation errors. Cold replay sometimes
differs for bf16 and FP8; the fractions below describe the completed runs of each
configuration, not a guarantee about later runs.

**The campaign's smoke test failed its MoE routing-count check, and the planned
repeat matrix is incomplete.** The failed check is retained in
[smoke_m1.txt](2026-09-15-h100-e6-modern/smoke_m1.txt). CUDA graph replay bypasses the
Python routing wrapper, leaving its diagnostic counts absent. Schedule, consumed
tokens, teacher forcing and output hashes pass the E6 checks independently of that
diagnostic. These results are recovered observations, not a passed ten-point smoke
test or an approved configuration list.

## Stack and method

The code is frozen at `7dc6f469726d3eec0719c98e9bf6458945b961af`. Every process reports
vLLM 0.29.0, torch 2.13.0+cu130, Triton 3.7.1, FlashInfer 0.6.18.post1 and driver
580.126.09 on NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3. Engine logs report FlashAttention 3. Each record
and replay has the same recorded package set. The four pods comprise two single-GPU
pods, M1 in India and M2 in the US, and two two-GPU pods, M3 in the US and M4 in
India. Cold replays cross those pairs in both directions; locations and GPU
identities are recorded in `machine_*.txt` and the arm metadata. A pod id alone
does not establish a different physical host.

All runs use the V1 runner, prefix caching off and synchronous scheduling. The
`v2` in legacy arm directory names denotes the revised experimental naming scheme,
not the model runner. Compiled arms resolve compilation mode 3 and
`FULL_AND_PIECEWISE` graphs; the uncompiled MoE arm resolves mode 0 and `FULL`.
TP=2 uses `NCCL_NVLS_ENABLE=0`. The plain schedule admits eight requests, then eight
more after five scheduler calls; the mixed schedule adds longer prompts and a
128-token prefill budget. Each continuation has 32 tokens.

The queue clears its enumerated cache roots and both temporary directories before
each cold start, refusing to proceed unless its file count is zero. All ten cold
records and 27 cold replays have zero shell counts, empty engine-side cache
snapshots and no direct AOT-cache-load message. This attests the checked locations.
It does not establish that every possible cache was observed. Warm replays retain
their record's caches; the restored replay copies the record's compiled caches.

## Two controls and a boundary rebuild

The determinism control sets all three of:

```text
TORCHINDUCTOR_DETERMINISTIC=1
--inductor-config '{"combo_kernels":false,"benchmark_combo_kernel":false}'
```

The variable and both resolved settings match in the record and three replays.
All five archived autotune configurations, excluding benchmark timing, match, as
do the extracted Triton function signatures and recorded extern-call and kernel
family counts. The combined configuration starts successfully and replays
identically in these three trials. **This does not isolate a remedy:** its plain
bf16 baseline also matched 3/3, and no mixed-schedule control was run. It does show
that disabling both combo settings avoids the startup failure observed when C1
used the determinism variable with the default combo configuration.

The batch-invariant control sets `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT=1` on both sides. It also
resolves `CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG=:16:8`, Tree/Simple NCCL settings, one channel,
NVLS off and custom all-reduce disabled. Its single cold replay differs in all
512 rows. The remaining two repeats were not run. This observation does not
measure a general failure frequency or identify which implementation caused it.

One bf16 plain replay after restoring the record's caches is IDENTICAL. One
boundary rebuild at pass 12, on the record's caches, differs in all 16 rows with
argmax unchanged. That is one boundary trial. Rebuilding prefixes with one
prefill changes the computational history, so it does not isolate the cause of
the changed bits or measure equality of KV tensors.

## Missing routing observations and rank limits

The hook clears its MoE count fields before every forward. Its Python router
wrapper can populate them during ordinary execution; a captured CUDA graph runs
without invoking that wrapper. All 37 forward passes of the MoE arm dispatch
FULL graphs and have null routing counts. The same pattern is present in the
earlier 0.28.0 uncompiled graph-enabled E6 arm. This is a source-and-log explanation
of missing diagnostics, not a new GPU validation of a repaired hook. No routing
counts are inferred or filled in, and the smoke result stays FAIL.

The headline comparator reads rank 0. Separate rank-1 comparisons give the same
verdict and differing-row count in all seven TP=2 comparisons. Within individual
runs, hidden states differ between ranks in four rows of the TP=2 plain record
and its warm replay, seven rows of plain cold repeat b, and five rows of mixed
cold repeat c. Full logits and argmax agree between ranks throughout. A matching
replay does not imply that the ranks have equal hidden states.

All 160 recorded continuations were decoded using tokenizers at the recorded
model revisions. They contain short continuations and ordinary prompt repetition,
without the earlier malformed compiled-MoE output. This checks obvious output
failure, not model quality. The [decoded text and tokenizer digests](2026-09-15-h100-e6-modern/decoded_text.json) are retained; `decode.py` regenerates them from the pinned public tokenizers. [Rank checks](2026-09-15-h100-e6-modern/rank_checks.json) regenerate with `audit_ranks.py` using the frozen comparator.
