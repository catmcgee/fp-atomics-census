The campaign was audited independently before attachment; the verdict was attach with edits, and the edits are applied in the commits that add this note and the matching README, runbook and hypothesis text. Every number here comes from the arm records, the pod's engine logs, RunPod's billing records or the audit.

## Model choices

- **Qwen3-8B-FP8 runs its checkpoint's blockwise FP8.** The checkpoint's `quantization_config` is `quant_method fp8`, `fmt e4m3`, `activation_scheme dynamic`, `weight_block_size [128, 128]`. The arm passed `--quantization fp8`, which matches the checkpoint (hence `fp8` in the arm name; this is not the online FP8 quantisation of a bf16 model used by the E4 `_fp8_` arms). Both engine logs print `Selected DeepGemmFp8BlockScaledMMKernel for Fp8LinearMethod` (vLLM's vendored DeepGEMM) and `DeepGEMM E8M0 enabled on current platform`. In the pinned vLLM source that kernel accepts only dynamic activation quantisation per token and per group of 128 channels, and the E8M0 setting gives power-of-two scales; the 0.28.0 wheel was not inspected. It is not a per-token FP8 arm.
- **Llama 3.1 8B Instruct comes from the NousResearch mirror.** The gated `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` repository returned 403 to the available Hugging Face token when the audit checked it, so the ungated mirror was the right choice. According to the Hugging Face API the mirror's four safetensors shards have the same SHA-256 as meta-llama's at `0e9e39f249a16976918f6564b8830bc894c89659`, and `config.json`, `generation_config.json`, `model.safetensors.index.json`, `special_tokens_map.json` and `tokenizer.json` are the same blobs; only `tokenizer_config.json` (the chat template) differs, and E6 passes raw prompts. The pod did not hash the files it loaded.
- **Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B-Chat runs uncompiled**, `--no-compile --cudagraph 0`, because torch.compile makes this model's output degenerate on this stack (see the hook-free check in the earlier note). MoE backend `TRITON Unquantized MoE backend`, `TritonExperts`, `MoEPrepareAndFinalizeNoDPEPModular`, as in the earlier E4 arms.

## What the replays were

Each replay was a new process: a separate invocation started after the record process had exited, with its own engine start-up, weight load and memory profile, its own `run_id` naming the record's, the record files' digests stored in its `run.json`, and request-id suffixes different from the record's in all 16 requests. In the Llama arm the replay's KV pool came out one 16-token block smaller than the record's (450,944 against 450,960 tokens); the pool size is not a comparison field and the replay was still IDENTICAL. The replays ran on the same pod straight after their records, with warm on-disk caches: the compiled arms' engine logs show the graph compile at 17.87 s and 5.56 s in the records and 0.45 s and 0.29 s in the replays, and the FP8 arm's DeepGEMM warm-up at 11.83 and 3,073.54 kernels per second. `VLLM_DISABLE_COMPILE_CACHE=1` was in effect (every compiled log prints `vLLM's torch.compile cache is disabled.` and none contains `Directly load AOT compilation`), but that variable covers vLLM's own compiled-artefact cache, not Inductor's or DeepGEMM's. The replays therefore show that a new process on the same pod reproduced the recorded passes from the record; they do not show that a cold recompilation would.

## Output check

Every recorded prompt re-tokenises to its admitted prompt ids with the tokenizer at the pinned revision. In all three arms every request has 32 tokens, no token repeats consecutively, no continuation ends in a cycle of period 3 or less, and replay tokens equal record tokens in 16 of 16 requests. The eight requests whose prompt repeats one sentence ("The verifier records the batch shape at every step and replays it later.") continue by repeating it, a 15-token period, in all three models; the other eight read as ordinary continuations, a description of the decoded strings rather than a measurement. The MoE arm's first request produces the same 32 tokens as the original-batch target of both uncompiled E4 MoE arms, not the compiled arms' two-token cycle.
